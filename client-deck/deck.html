Concept · Decision Layer
AI-Assisted Planner — Rules-Driven Route Planning
COTRA AUTOTRANSPORT AG
Dispatcher Workbench — Demo
🚚 Truck Rules
Per-truck operational limits
  • Deck position & configuration (upper / lower)
  • Height limits per slot (e.g. 1.55m upper deck)
  • Weight limits & axle balance
  • Loading sequence (LIFO by stop order)
🗺 Route Rules
Shared constraints for every route
  • Route geography & dealer sequence
  • Split-route & transfer leg logic
  • Country compliance (CH 4.0m / EU 4.3m)
  • Example: Route 2 → Geneva → Lucerne
👤 Driver Rules
Duty & assignment limits
  • Driver availability
  • Max working hours / day
  • Remaining hours before rest
  • Truck pairing & legal rest compliance
🏗 Compound Rules
Yard & staging constraints
  • Staging lane allocation
  • Lane capacity & vehicle sequence
  • Loading schedule & yard prep time
  • Vehicle location tracking
⚡
AI-Assisted Planner
Combines all four rule sets to auto-plan routes,
trucks & deck loading — flagging violations
before dispatch.
Today, this logic exists only in dispatchers' heads. The AI-assisted planner codifies it into a constraint engine that proposes a compliant plan in seconds — the dispatcher reviews and confirms.
